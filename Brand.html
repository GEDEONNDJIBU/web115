Mongoose Restaurant Brand Guide

Brand Concept

Mongoose Restaurant is a casual restaurant specializing in delicious chicken while also offering a variety of other foods. The brand focuses on providing flavorful, affordable meals in a welcoming environment for individuals, friends, and families.

Mission Statement

Our mission is to provide customers with delicious and affordable meals made with care. Mongoose Restaurant aims to become a place where customers can enjoy quality chicken and a variety of flavorful foods while receiving friendly service.

Color Scheme

Dark Red (#8B1E1E): Used for headings, the header, and important elements.

Gold (#D4A017): Used for links, accents, and highlights.

Font Choices

Georgia: Used for headings and the restaurant name.

Arial: Used for paragraphs and general website content.

Tagline

"Great Taste, Every Bite."
